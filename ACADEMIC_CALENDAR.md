## Assessments

The StudentOS model includes the three assessment milestones requested for the semester:

1. **IA-1 — Internal Assessment 1**
2. **IA-2 — Internal Assessment 2**
3. **SEE — Semester End Examination**

Exact dates are intentionally left as pending until an official RUAS examination/academic calendar is available. Do not infer exam dates from the weekly timetable.

## Break behavior

Tea Break and Lunch Break are first-class timetable events. They should appear in the day timeline, be excluded from class-count calculations, and be recognized by the live/next-state engine.
